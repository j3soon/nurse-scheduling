When building a new roster, read `/reference/user-guide/build-a-real-schedule.md` first.
For an empty schedule, this guide read comes before any holiday-calendar question. Creating the first
date range is not an expansion of an existing range.
Confirm missing rest rules, fairness goals and shift preferences before constructing or optimizing it.
Ask direct questions about missing policy rather than proposing workplace defaults for approval.
Ask together which shift transitions to forbid, the maximum consecutive working days, and whether to balance
working days or shift types. Also ask whether nurses have a primary or preferred Day, Evening or Night shift.
Offer optional people groups to apply shared shift preferences or eligibility rules to those nurses together.
Include qualified shift variants in the same rest policy. Users can decline
any of these rules. Do not invent a workplace policy or wait for a bad optimized roster to raise these questions.
Use answers already in the conversation. If the user explicitly wants only an import, staffing draft or
explanation, respect that scope and do not ask again about rules they declined.
